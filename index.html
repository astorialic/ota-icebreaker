How OTA Icebreaker works

1 · Players join

Everyone opens the app and taps Play. They enter their name, optionally add a photo, then answer 8 questions.

2 · Run matching

Once enough players have joined, tap Run matching on the Overview tab. Each player gets 4 matches based on their answers.

3 · Find your matches

Players see their 4 matches with how many things they have in common. They go find each person in the room and have a real conversation.

4 · Claim points

After talking, tap a match card and write one thing you learned about them. If they confirm it — you both get a point.

5 · Win

First person to reach 5 points wins. Check the Scores tab for the live leaderboard.

Host tips

  • Wait until at least 10 players have joined before running matching
  • Announce "matching is live" so everyone checks their phones
  • Speed date decks are under the 🎯 tab on each player's game screen
  • Use Clear room to wipe all player data and start fresh
